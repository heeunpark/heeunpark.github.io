Research

Fields: Technology and Innovation Management; Strategic Management; Organization Theory

My research investigates why organizations facing similar technological changes pursue different strategic trajectories. I examine how evolving problem representations shape the direction, scope, and pace of capability search, the configuration of strategic actions, and the durability of organizational commitments. My current work explores these relationships through longitudinal analyses of corporate technological interpretations, external capability search, and strategic responses to emerging technologies.

Methods: panel-data and fixed-effects models; multinomial logistic regression; semi-structured literature review; Delphi studies and stakeholder interviews; quantitative content analysis and LLM-assisted content coding. Tools: Python, R, SQL.
